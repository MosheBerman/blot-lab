## Flagged: blocks of deleted/redacted text

### A. Scan 17, message side, lower-left quadrant — BLACK INK BLOCK
- **Location:** bottom-left of the message area, just above the closing
  line "Küsse Euch vielmals".
- **Size:** roughly 1.5 cm tall × 6 cm wide, covering ~2 lines of handwriting.
- **Appearance:** heavy black scribble, fully opaque — the writer's own
  pen blacking out what was written. Distinct from the letter-39 blot
  (which is a blue wash); this is deliberate obliteration with dark ink.
- **Candidate for:** shade-isolation passes (dark ink on dark ink is hard,
  but the block edges may preserve stroke ghosts).

### B. Scan 51, message side, scattered — BLUE INK DABS over words
- **Location:** ~5–6 distinct blue ink blobs across the small card:
  one top-right of center, one mid-right, one large blob bottom-right,
  plus smaller dabs sitting directly on words mid-line (e.g. over "wird").
- **Size:** individual blobs ~0.5–1.5 cm.
- **Appearance:** same blue ink as the writing; blobs sit on top of text
  words. Could be a leaky pen, but placement over words suggests
  deliberate dabbing-out. The large bottom-right blob covers several
  characters.
- **Candidate for:** shade-isolation passes; the dabs are the same hue as
  the text, so chroma separation (a suggested extension) matters here.

## Method note

50-dpi contact sheet of all 54 PDF first pages → visual triage for
postcard format (small card, stamps, postmarks, divided back, "Postkarte"
imprint) → 150-dpi inspection of 9 candidates. One candidate (50) turned
out to be a receipt; one (10) a small letter. 7 postcards confirmed,
2 flagged for redaction-block analysis.
