### Known examples re-confirmed (do not re-report)

  (Steiermark/Styria with an extra *t*).

  (Nazi Gau name, spelled correctly).

## Note on scope

The writers name real places plainly and often spell them correctly
(Wien, Strzyzow, Lublin, Opole, Wiznitz, Lisboa, Jugoslawien, Tel-Aviv,
Chicago, N. Y., Amerika, Polen, Rumänien). The odd-spelling-for-place
pattern in this corpus is limited to the four known cases above. The
coded-language angle in these letters lives in the Hebrew/Yiddish
insertions, abbreviations, and phrasing — not in disguised place names.
