L18 | Chaja Willner, Vienna 7 Oct 1940, postcard to Jakob Willner
i visible: matches Helen. Two violet boxed stamps "282" and "23b" (Helen fn2) under sender address = censor examiner numbers; Wehrmacht-censor eagle stamp. "Rfua schlajma bkerew" written in Latin letters with "bkerew" added beneath as a correction. Coloured flag over "Ewa und Maly B. den Brief" = a corrected line ("und Maly B." squeezed in).
ii lighter: card is curved in the photo (light falloff on the left half); within lines, no isolated faint word.
iii marks: one brown spot right of "dass" (foxing).
Plain-sight check: "vielleicht wird der Schwager zu Jom Kipur uns besuchen" — the "brother-in-law" (Josef) visiting = a recurring way to say Josef's whereabouts/leave (also L11 "Josef wird uns fileicht besuchen", "had no time at Yom Kippur"). HIAS notified of 50 dollars; "der Am. K. gibt kein Visum" — direct, not coded.
