i visible: matches Helen. Corner "3/30" (examiner). "G-t" written without the o (Helen fn2) — deliberate pious abbreviation, also "G.s.d." and "Gt soll helfen". "schliesse" overwritten on "Schreiben", crossed "grüsse" on the last page.
ii lighter: copying-pencil tone shifts (violet when wet, grey when dry) visible across lines; no isolated faint word. The hue map is uniformly violet → one instrument.
iii marks: specks along the fold.
Plain-sight location: "Ich befinde mich immer auf derselben Ortschaft wie früher" (still in the same village as before) — refers back to an earlier letter that named or described the place (L12/L26: near Schlojme). Requests for old clothes and food, "don't send cash" — consistent with a work camp or ghetto under the General Government. 29 Jul 1941 was a Tuesday — the weekday matches the date (writer is precise).
