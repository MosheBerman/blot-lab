L31 | Malka (Mali) Bermann, Palestine, early Dec 1940 (two letters on one sheet, numbered "I"), to Uncle Jakob
i visible: matches Helen. Hebrew headings "ב"ה יום א' …" (Sunday + Hebrew date) at the top of each letter and short Hebrew blessings after names ("Onkel Jakob נ"י"). "G-tt" written without the o ("Gott" → "G'tt", "mit G-ttes Hilfe"). Superscript "mit" inserted. Two holes (paper loss) at the centre fold.
ii lighter: none; even ink.
iii marks: none systematic.
Content: Mommy has the summons to the consular examination but "they will only see people who already have a ship ticket"; "children left for America on 1 December"; "Toten Bankes" (Yiddish idiom, Helen fn1); "1000000 x … 1000" and "100000000000000000000000" kisses (23 zeros). Plain statement, no disguise. Numbers: book-cipher reading of number pairs gave nonsense ("jetzt").
