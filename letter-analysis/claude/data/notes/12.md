L12 | Josef Bermann, undated (1940–41), to Eva — copying (indelible) pencil on thin paper
i visible: matches Helen (heavy show-through; Helen fn1–3). Superscript insertion "gefahren" above "Quote … und von hier".
ii lighter / different tone: two tones of the same instrument — violet (moistened copying pencil) for most of p1–p2, and a grey-green dry graphite tone for the closing lines of p1 ("…Quote gefahren und von hier ist noch gar niemand … gebe G.t daß es … soll fahren können") and the last lines of p2. A copying pencil writes grey when dry and violet when wet, so a tone switch means a pause/re-wetting, not necessarily a second writer; letterforms match. The tone change happens exactly at the "nobody from here has left" sentence — recorded, not over-read.
iii marks: two brown spots (foxing) flagged; no dot pattern.
Plain-sight location (WWI convention match): "ich befinde mich in der Nähe deines Bruders Schlojme" — tells Eva where he is by reference to a relative (Schlojme = Salomon Willner, Strzyżów). Also "ich kann nicht selbst aufgeben" (he cannot post letters himself) — signals a closed camp/controlled mail. Strong candidate for a deliberate censorship-evading statement.
