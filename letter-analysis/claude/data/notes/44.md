L44 | Owadie Bermann, Tel Aviv, 29 March (c. 1941), to sister Rosa and brother-in-law Gerson; second letter to sister-in-law (Eva)
i visible: matches Helen. Brown ink on browned paper; tears at the folds.
ii lighter: none.
iii marks: specks along tears.
Euphemism/plain-sight (strong): "Hoffentlich hat der Josef doch welche Möglichkeiten her zu kommen ... gebe der Herrgott, die sollen schon alle ausgelest werden mit einer guten Hilf. Amen." — Helen fn4 reads "ausgelest" as ausgelöst = ransomed/freed. "Auslösen" is also the word for redeeming captives (pidyon shvuyim); Owadie, writing from British Palestine, is free to say it. Also "Parnuse" (parnasa, livelihood) in Hebrew-German.
Lead: Saul Waldbaum (NY), Dr Perlrot letter, "Auszug unserer Sachen" (list of belongings — possibly the Vermögensanmeldung or a lift-van inventory) held by Berta.
