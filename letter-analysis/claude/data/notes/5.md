L5 | Salomon Willner, Strzyżów 28 Apr 1941, postcard to Jakob Willner
i visible: matches Helen. Red censor stamp on address side (orange in hue map) with offset red specks on message side right of "S. Willner" (transfer, not ink). Pencil "1220"/initials near the lower edge = handling marks. Printed "11.41".
ii lighter / different ink: the postscript lines ("bekommt Ihr schreiben fon Etlen", "obiger", "habe Josef geschickt 20 zł, den er hat ferlangt fon mir. S.", "bitte antwort") are written heavier and slightly bluer than the body — same pen re-loaded for an afterthought (consistent hand and letterforms); Helen fn1 notes "Ojwiger/obiger" is squeezed between lines. Body itself shows no faint word.
iii marks: the paired dots after "ferdient", "Kalmen", "gesund" sit exactly where questions end — the writer's question mark, not a dot code (same habit absent from non-question sentences).
Content leads: "Josef schreibt fon Lublin das er ferdient" (Apr 1941) — places Josef in Lublin district two months after the Feb 1941 Vienna transports; 20 zł sent to Josef on request.
