i visible: matches Helen. Corner "1834" (examiner). 8 Sep 1941 was a Monday — date checks out.

Plain-sight (strong, decoder for the whole 1941 set): "es ist nicht wegen Schreibfaulheit, sondern ich muss erst an Chajcze schreiben und sie befördert weiter" — Josef cannot write to America directly; his letters go via Chaja (still in Vienna — L52 is dated Vienna 16 Sep 1941). Combined with L12 "I can't post letters myself", this is the mail route: Josef → Chaja (Vienna) → New York. "da machst Du mich beim Leben erhalten" (you would keep me alive) — direct statement of need for clothes.
