L52 | Chaja and Maly, Vienna 16 Sep 1941 (Rosh Hashana greetings), to siblings — top edge "1837/2 v2158" (Helen; the image reads "1834/2 … 2157"; cf. L48 corner "1834", Josef 8 Sep 1941 — same examiner run if 1834)
i visible: matches Helen. Translation nuance (own): "ich zahle allein den Zins" — in Austrian German "Zins" = rent, so the sense is "I pay the rent by myself, thank G-d", not "only paying the interest". "um uns herunter zu nehmen" (Helen fn4): Viennese "herunternehmen/herüberholen" — to bring us over.
ii lighter: none. iii marks: none systematic.
Plain-sight (strong): "Ich habe gedacht, der Josef wird mich zum Feiertag besuchen, leider [nicht]" — Josef (Opole) cannot travel; "visiting" is the family's word for movement between Vienna and the east (cf. L11, L18, L43). "Bitte, wenn Du kannst, nur alte Wäsche/Kleider an Salomon für Josef schicken, sonst nichts" — the route to Josef is via Salomon (Strzyżów), as in L51 and L34. "Chanale visited me" — unknown person; possible lead.
