L17 | Chaja Willner, Vienna 18 Jul 1940 (postmark 19.7.40), postcard to Jakob Willner
i visible: matches Helen. Stamped "1517" under the sender name (examiner/handling number); pencil "1268–1345" lower edge. A word corrected over another at "for die Ostern" (Helen fn2: remains of another letter before the f) — the overwrite is visible as a heavier, re-formed letter, not a hidden word. Superscript "ein Brief" inserted above "forige Woche". Black blackout over ≈2.5 lines after "soll die Ewa und Kinder mir schreiben", before "sonst Grüße".
Block test (8 views: R/G/B, CLAHE, dark-end stretch, blue-minus-red, black-hat): CLAHE shows only the card's printed ruling as short light dashes running through the block and the postmark ring passing across it; no stroke-shaped structure inside. Blue-minus-red: the block is bluer than the surrounding brown writing — but the same shift occurs for the writer's own thick strokes and for deletions elsewhere (letter 1), so it does not prove a second pen. Order of postmark vs blackout: indeterminate (both black).
ii lighter: none; ink uniform.
iii marks: specks around the violet censor eagle stamp = stamp ink.
Context of the cut: it follows "my brother-in-law can't write himself because his fingers hurt" — an excuse for Josef not writing ("er ist noch dort" = he is still there, Helen fn1). The adjacent sentence is a classic plain-sight signal (a body-ailment excuse for absence). What was removed was most likely information about Josef or about the writer's own departure ("ich weiß nicht wann ich daran komme nach USA").
