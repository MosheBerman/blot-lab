Korn on the reverse: water droplets dissolved the copying-pencil dye and carried it through. Black-hat view shows Josef's strokes continuing under the drip ("Ich hab[e] schon in Amerika ...") — covered, not destroyed; Helen's reading stands.
ii lighter: no isolated faint word in Josef's hand.
iii marks: the many specks on p2 are dye spatter from the same wetting.
Content: "Mrs Dornhelm hopefully leaving in 14 days, already has the visa"; "Taube Ettel's Peretz going to America soon"; "Ruchel Lea is staying with me"; kisses "10000000000000000" (16 zeros; date 11) — plain.
