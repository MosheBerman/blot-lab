i visible: matches Helen. Post-war Israeli aerogramme; printed blue Hebrew security pattern (דואר אויר / מדינת ישראל) covers every panel. Two scans (6-002/6-003) show the same panel twice. Blue water/ink stain over the sender address = Helen fn7. [Hebrew/Yiddish] items: "Ka"h", a Hebrew word for "joy/nachas" — ordinary.
ii lighter: ballpoint, uniform pressure; faint areas = the overprinted security pattern and fold lines. Hue: one ballpoint (violet-blue) per writer; Susi/Sali section same pen family.
iii marks: specks belong to the printed pattern; nothing under letters.
Era note: 1952, no wartime censorship — included for completeness. "law now that you can only send something once every six months" = Israeli austerity (tzena) export rules, a datable reference.
