L23 | Chaja Willner, Vienna 13 Aug 1940, postcard to Ewa Berman
i visible: matches Helen. Stamp "1517" (same examiner number as L17) and "1532[?]" on the address side; pencil "2511–1399a" lower edge; two Wehrmacht-censor eagles. Message runs up the left edge of the address side.
ii lighter: hot-spot glare in the photo lightens the centre of the message side; strokes inside it are not individually lighter. No faint word.
iii marks: three small red specks on the message side = foxing.
Content: "my brother-in-law writes often, he is doing well" — Josef referred to as "Schwager" (brother-in-law) by Chaja; consistent with her role as go-between ("Josef gave me a letter to send to you" — letters routed through her). Ship tickets "über Lisabon" — Lisbon route, verifiable in HIAS/JDC files.
