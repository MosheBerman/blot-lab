i visible: own reading of the dabbed line where Helen has [unclear] (fn2, fn3): "der Josef glaubt fon dort wird schwerer nach U.S.A. zu kommen. Er könnt a paar M[ark] zum Leben brauchen, vielleicht könnt man zu Schlojmen schicken, er soll ihm geben — fon hier könnt man ihm nicht [es] schicken." The "apar M." before the dab = "a paar Mark" (a few marks): the request is for money, not food parcels (resolves Helen fn2 toward "financial help"). Money cannot be sent from Vienna to the General Government; Schlojme (Strzyżów) can pass it on in złoty — consistent with L5 ("sent Josef 20 zł he asked for").
Dab test: the blue dabs sit on the line's words but are the same blue as the ink (one pen); round drops plus a large drop lower right — a leaking pen on blotting-prone card, not deliberate cover (they also fall on harmless words like "brauchen" and "schicken").
Red specks over "Lily H[ö]nig": a separate red pigment lying on top of the blue ink, ~6 small spots — not writing; possibly transfer from a red-stamped item. Recorded, not interpreted.
